Let x be a positive integer and let eta1 be the trapezoidal cutoff from
Section 8 of Tao's five-primes paper. Set

$$M_x=\sum_{n=0}^{x}\Lambda(n)\mathbf1_{(n,\sqrt{x}\#)=1}\eta_1(n/x)^2.$$

Here the square-root primorial includes primes at most the integer square
root of x, exactly as in the platform definition. Assume

$$10^8\le x/10,\qquad 10^4(3/2)\le x,\qquad
5(3/2)\le\log(x/10),\qquad 10^8(9/4)\le x.$$

Then there is a real epsilon such that

$$|\varepsilon|\le0.02,\qquad M_x=\frac23(1+\varepsilon)x.$$

This is the quadratic prime-mass input (4.13) of Corollary 4.9 specialized
to the Section 8 cutoff. The factor 2/3 is its squared L2 norm; it must not
be replaced by 1. The explicit prime estimate, removal of prime powers,
and passage from the smooth statement to the literal piecewise-linear
cutoff remain part of this theorem's proof obligation. This statement
does not assert a major-arc energy lower bound.
