Under the hypotheses of the unit-numerator case of Tao's Theorem 5.1,
the smoothed sum satisfies

$$
|S_{\eta_0,2}(x,\alpha)|\le B_I+B_{II},
$$

where

$$
B_I=\frac{96}{\pi^2}\frac{x}{(x/q)^2}\log(4x)
\log\left(\frac{4eq}{\pi}\right)
$$

and

$$
B_{II}=\left(0.1\frac{x}{\sqrt q}+0.39\frac{x}{\sqrt{x/q}}\right)
\log\frac{x}{UV}\log\frac{Vx}{U}
+\left(0.55\frac{x}{\sqrt U}+0.78\frac{x}{\sqrt V}\right)\log\frac{x}{U}.
$$

This reduction separates three substantive obligations, corresponding to
Tao's Lemma 4.11 and Sections 5.2 and 5.3. The imported interface contains
only the concrete sums and coefficient definitions, not their estimates.

The Vaughan child supplies a coefficient family of modulus at most one
and bounds the smoothed sum by its Type I envelope plus the centered Type II
sum. The Type I child bounds that envelope uniformly in the coefficient
family by the first displayed term. The Type II child bounds the literal
bilinear sum by the remaining two terms. Adding the two inequalities gives
the exact target, including all original constants and parameter hypotheses.

This submission is a reduction: it does not prove the Vaughan or Type II
children. The Type I child has a separate complete proof submission.
Resolving all three children is necessary to complete the parent.
