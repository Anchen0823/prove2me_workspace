The proof is a pointwise identity for natural-number truncated subtraction. Omega checks the three cases v≥11a, 2a≤v<11a, and v<2a; casting to the reals, multiplying by arbitrary weights, and summing gives the exact formula. There is no positivity assumption on the weights because this is equality. The statement is only the abstract finite core of the local prime-discount identity; it does not prove the concrete ζ(9) valuation estimates or the open discounted asymptotic target.
