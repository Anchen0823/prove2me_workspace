Suppose $D,s,N,g,\Delta,\Xi$ are real numbers with $D,s,N,g,\Xi>0$ and

$$
\Delta=\frac{s^2N}{D^2}\,\Xi.
$$

Then the logarithmic volume baseline satisfies

$$
\log(D/s)+\tfrac12\log\Delta-\tfrac12\log g
=\tfrac12\bigl(\log\Xi+\log(N/g)\bigr).
$$

The area identity expresses the change of a two-dimensional covolume under a matrix of determinant $s^2N$ and scaling by $D^{-1}$. Positivity permits the logarithm laws. Taking logarithms of the hypothesis gives

$$
\log\Delta=2\log s+\log N-2\log D+\log\Xi.
$$

Substitution cancels $\log D$ and $\log s$, leaving the claimed expression. This theorem formalizes the cancellation conditional on the area identity; the concrete construction of $\Delta_n$ and $\Xi_n$ from the zeta coefficient matrix is a separate formalization target.
