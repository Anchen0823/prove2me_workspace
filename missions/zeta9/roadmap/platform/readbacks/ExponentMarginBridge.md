For any functions \(B,\sigma:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R\), suppose there is a real number \(\beta<2641/250\) such that \(B(2k+2)\leq\beta\) for all sufficiently large natural numbers \(k\), and suppose \(\sigma(2k+2)\to 0\) as \(k\to\infty\). Then there is a real number \(\varepsilon>0\) such that \(B(2k+2)+\sigma(2k+2)\leq 2641/250-\varepsilon\) for all sufficiently large natural numbers \(k\). The hypotheses impose no conditions on the functions at indices outside the sequence \(2,4,6,\ldots\).
