For every $n\ge0$,
$$d_{2n}A_n\in\mathbb Z.$$
Every integer between $1$ and $m\le N$ divides $d_N$. Induction on $m$ therefore shows that $d_NH_m$ is an integer: the new term is $d_N/(m+1)$, also an integer. Apply this with $N=2n$ and $m=n+i$ for $0\le i\le n$, multiply by the integer $\binom ni^2$, and sum. The case $n=0$ is included. This proof uses no open platform theorem.
