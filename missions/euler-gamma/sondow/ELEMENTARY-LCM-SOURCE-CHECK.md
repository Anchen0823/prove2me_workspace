# Bounded search for an elementary replacement

The required coefficient is `log(8)/2 = 3*log(2)/2`, approximately 1.03972077084. A bound with coefficient 1.04, the classical Chebyshev upper coefficient 1.10555, or `lcmUpto m <= 3^m` does not imply the exact scaled-integral target. A useful eventual replacement also needs an explicit threshold and a proved treatment of every smaller argument.

A bounded Sol source check found no directly usable theorem with all the required constants and an explicit threshold at most `10^8`. This is a search result, not a nonexistence result.

The Diamond–Erdős paper is a possible methodology reference, but no concrete parameters and effective threshold sufficient for this task were established by the check. Source: https://combinatorica.hu/~p_erdos/1981-11.pdf . Do not import an asymptotic statement as the required all-argument estimate.
