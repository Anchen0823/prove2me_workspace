For each fixed $n\ge0$,
$$E_{n,N}\longrightarrow0.$$
For any fixed nonnegative integer $a$, induction using the harmonic-number recurrence proves
$$H_{N+a}-H_N\longrightarrow0.$$
Each induction step adds one reciprocal of a shifted natural number, which tends to zero. Also $a/N$ tends to zero, and continuity of the logarithm at $1$ yields
$$\log(1+a/N)\longrightarrow0.$$
The correction is a finite sum of these sequences with coefficients independent of $N$. Finite sums, constant multiples, and addition preserve their zero limits. No integral evaluation or arithmetic conjecture is used.
