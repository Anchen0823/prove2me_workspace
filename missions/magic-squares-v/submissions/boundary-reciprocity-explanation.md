This submission gives a new conditional proof of semi-magic reciprocity. Its only unproved imported theorem is `MagicSquares.matching_boundary_euler`; it does not claim a complete proof of the mission.

For a board B, define a(B) by Boolean inclusion-exclusion of the predicate that B contains a permutation. The imported finite theorem states a weighted boundary cancellation: if B and D are nonempty matching-covered boards, D is contained in B, and the permutation support phi is contained in B, then the sum of a(C) over D contained in C contained in B with B minus phi contained in C is a(B) when phi is contained in D, and zero otherwise.

The submitted Lean source proves all subsequent steps. It identifies a(B) with the exact-support counting polynomial at -1, converts finite boundary cancellation into a polynomial boundary identity by partitioning matrices according to support, and proves normalized reflection by induction on board size using first differences and a base value. Translation by the all-ones matrix identifies the full-support polynomial with the semi-magic counting polynomial. Comparison of leading coefficients supplies the reflection sign, and the previously established exact degree gives parity n-1. Polynomial identity then yields the requested formula for every integer argument.

The new child has a classical mathematical justification via the Eulerian face lattice of the Birkhoff polytope and the order-dual form of Weisner's theorem. The specialization is derived by us, not quoted as a statement from Stanley: in the interval [D,B], the cancellation condition is C join (D join phi) = B. The corresponding formal Lean proof remains open.

Reference for that proof route: Richard P. Stanley, Enumerative Combinatorics, Volume 1, author manuscript, Proposition 3.8.9 (p. 309) and Corollary 3.9.3 (p. 313, applied to the order dual), https://math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/ec1.pdf. These references motivate the remaining finite theorem; the submission does not import any reciprocity or Ehrhart result to prove itself.
